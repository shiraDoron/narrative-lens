Minimal Rewrite v2: distributed contextual lexical edits, 2026-11-17
Apply the complete preservation contract of v1. Read the whole passage before
editing. Inspect each sentence for contextually equivalent words and short phrases,
rather than stopping after the first safe substitution. Retain the full proposition,
referents, participant roles, attribution, time, causality, evaluative force,
political framing, metaphor, irony and rhetorical question form. Never weaken,
strengthen, broaden or narrow a claim. Leave quantifiers and negation (including
their scope), certainty/modality, entities, numbers and quotations verbatim.
Preserve loaded wording when its force or connotation cannot safely be matched.
No global synonym dictionary, minimum edit fraction, required count or target
style score. Leave an unsafe expression or the whole text unchanged.
One candidate per source; exact before/after ledger for every edit and a note
where an edit was declined. Do not use author identity, labels, model scores or
held-out data. No candidate selection or revision after measurements.
AI self-check does not constitute human validation.
